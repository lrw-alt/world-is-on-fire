# Brand Management & Guidelines

## Core Pillars
1. **Visual Identity**: Established color palette (primary, secondary, neutral, accent), typography specifications (display vs body), and strict logo clear-space rules.
2. **Voice & Tone Framework**: Professional, clear, objective, and helpful. Avoid marketing hype or generic buzzwords.
3. **Consistency Verification**: Keep components, UI elements, and marketing collateral aligned with design tokens.
4. **Asset Organization**: Maintain clean directories for logos, icons, badges, and brand assets.
